## Project Transformation | تحول المشروع
We have successfully transformed the **Finovate Audit Nexus AI** from a skeletal structure into a fully functional, secure, and professional enterprise-grade audit platform.

### Key Accomplishments | الإنجازات الرئيسية:

1.  **Enterprise Security | أمان المؤسسات**:
    - Full implementation of `bcrypt` hashing for passwords.
    - Secure key derivation and JWT management.
    - Production-ready CORS and exception handling.

2.  **Functional AI Agents | وكلاء ذكاء اصطناعي وظيفيون**:
    - **Chief Agent**: Orchestrates real parallel execution of sub-agents.
    - **Journal Agent**: Implements Benford's Law and statistical anomaly detection.
    - **Fraud Agent**: Advanced pattern recognition for fraud indicators.
    - **Compliance Agent**: Automated mapping to IFRS and Egyptian accounting standards.

3.  **Robust Backend & Integration | واجهة خلفية وتكامل قوي**:
    - Standardized `BaseERPConnector` for any ERP integration (SAP, Odoo, etc.).
    - `DatabaseManager` for persistent storage of financial data and findings.
    - `AuditService` to bridge the gap between raw data, AI analysis, and persistence.

4.  **Advanced Frontend | واجهة أمامية متقدمة**:
    - Professional web-based dashboard using `Chart.js`.
    - Real-time KPI monitoring and interactive findings table.
    - Full Arabic language support with accounting-specific terminology.

5.  **Intelligence Features | ميزات الذكاء**:
    - **Predictive Analysis**: Forecasting revenue trends and future risk scores.
    - **Automated Reporting**: Generation of professional executive summaries in Markdown.
