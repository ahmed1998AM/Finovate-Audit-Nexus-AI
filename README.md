## 🏗️ Architecture

### 1. Core Intelligence Layer
The central nucleus of the system including:
- AI Management
- Agent Management
- Context Management
- Memory Management
- Workflow Management
- Quality Assurance
- Event Management
- Risk Management

### 2. Multi-Agent AI System
22 Specialized AI Agents working independently, synchronously, and cooperatively:

#### Core Audit Agents
1. **Chief Audit AI Agent** - Master agent managing all others
2. **Journal Entry Audit Agent** - Detects duplicates, anomalies, manipulation
3. **General Ledger Audit Agent** - Analyzes movements and balances
4. **Trial Balance Audit Agent** - Verifies matching and balance
5. **Financial Statements Audit Agent** - Reviews all financial statements

#### Compliance & Tax Agents
6. **Tax Compliance AI Agent** - VAT, Income Tax, Payroll Tax, Withholding Tax
7. **Compliance & Standards Agent** - Egyptian standards, IFRS, ISA

#### Specialized Audit Agents
8. **Bank & Treasury Audit Agent** - Bank reconciliation, suspicious transactions
9. **Inventory Audit Agent** - Stock analysis, cost analysis
10. **Fixed Assets Audit Agent** - Depreciation, additions, disposals

#### Intelligence Agents
11. **Fraud Detection AI Agent** - Pattern detection, anomaly detection
12. **OCR & Document Intelligence Agent** - PDF, images, Word, Excel processing
13. **Behavioral Intelligence Agent** - User behavior analysis
14. **Risk Scoring Agent** - Risk, Fraud, Compliance scoring
15. **Forensic Accounting Agent** - Money tracing, fake invoices detection
16. **Explainable AI Agent** - Explains AI decisions
17. **AI Quality Assurance Agent** - Detects hallucinations, evaluates confidence
18. **Executive Intelligence Agent** - Performance analysis, insights
19. **ERP Connector Agent** - SAP, Oracle, Dynamics, Odoo integration
20. **Continuous Audit Agent** - Real-time monitoring
21. **Financial Graph Intelligence Agent** - Relationship analysis
22. **AI Copilot Agent** - Intelligent financial assistant
